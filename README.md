# 🔒 SentiMate — Privacy Policy & Data Governance
**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026  
**Application Name:** SentiMate Student Guidance Analytics & Support System  
---
## 1. Overview & Purpose
**SentiMate** is an educational mental health analytics and crisis triage system designed to support student well-being. SentiMate monitors authorized social media environments (such as school Facebook Pages and Groups), performs real-time Natural Language Processing (NLP) sentiment scoring, and provides confidential 24/7 AI-guided emotional support resources to students.
---
## 2. Information We Process
SentiMate collects and processes minimal data strictly necessary for mental health triage and guidance referral:
* **Social Media Post Content:** Text content published in authorized school feeds to evaluate sentiment scores (Positive, Neutral, Negative) and emotional categories (Anxiety, Sadness, Stress, Anger).
* **System Tokens:** Cryptographically generated one-time session tokens (`token_...`) used to transition students safely from social media posts to confidential 1-on-1 AI counseling sessions on the guidance website.
* **Counseling Interaction Logs:** Voluntary chatbot conversation logs generated when a student interacts with the AI Counselor on the official guidance website.
---
## 3. Student Anonymity & Data Protection Safeguards
SentiMate is built on a **Privacy-First Architecture**:
* **Zero Identity Harvesting:** SentiMate **does not** collect, harvest, or store personal Facebook profiles, friend lists, phone contacts, or private Facebook messages.
* **Tokenized Anonymity Layer:** Deep links provided by SentiMate utilize one-time anonymous session tokens. These tokens map post risk categories without linking or storing student personal identities.
* **Voluntary Identification:** Students retain full control over their identity. Real-world student credentials (such as Student ID or School Email) are requested **only** when a student explicitly requests an appointment with a human guidance counselor.
* **No Commercial Data Exploitation:** Student data is never sold, leased, rented, or monetized for advertising or third-party marketing purposes.
## 4. Compliance & Institutional Standards
SentiMate's data processing practices align with global student privacy and data protection standards:
* **Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA):** Protects student educational and counseling records.
* **Data Privacy Act of 2012 / General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):** Enforces data minimization, purpose limitation, and storage limitation.
---
## 5. Data Retention & Security
* All text processing occurs over encrypted HTTPS connections (`TLS 1.3`).
* In-memory session tokens automatically expire within 24 hours of creation.
* Analytics data stored for guidance counselors is anonymized and aggregated into general community well-being trend charts.
